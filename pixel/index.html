Recovering & Understanding
Human Motion in 4D

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computing (CIRCLE Group), Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Tolga Birdal, with Prof. Stefanos Zafeiriou. Previously, I completed an MRes in Computer Science at Durham University under Prof. Toby Breckon, and I was a research intern at Tencent AI Lab working with Dr. Shao-Li Huang and Dr. Jue Wang. My research focuses on 3D/4D computer vision with an emphasis on hand–object interaction, neural motion priors, and robust reconstruction systems for real-world applications.
