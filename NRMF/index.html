Abstract

▾
We introduce Neural Riemannian Motion Fields (NRMF), a novel 3D generative human motion prior that enables robust, temporally consistent, and physically plausible 3D motion recovery. Unlike existing VAE or diffusion-based methods, our higher-order motion prior explicitly models human motion in the zero-level set of a collection of neural distance fields (NDFs) corresponding to pose, transition (velocity), and acceleration dynamics. Our framework constructs these NDFs on the product space of joint rotations, their angular velocities, and angular accelerations, respecting the underlying geometry. We further introduce (i) a novel adaptive-step projection algorithm and (ii) a geometric integrator to roll out realistic trajectories during test-time optimization and generation, achieving consistent gains across tasks and inputs.

Method Overview

NRMF models plausible poses (\u03B8), transitions (\u02D9\u03B8), and accelerations (\u02DD\u03B8) via geometric neural distance fields, enabling projection and integration for denoising, in-the-wild estimation, in-betweening, and generation.
