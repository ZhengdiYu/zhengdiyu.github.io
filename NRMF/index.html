Abstract

▾
We introduce Neural Riemannian Motion Fields (NRMF), a novel 3D generative human motion prior that enables robust, temporally consistent, and physically plausible 3D motion recovery. Unlike existing VAE or diffusion-based methods, our higher-order motion prior explicitly models human motion in the zero-level set of a collection of neural distance fields (NDFs) corresponding to pose, transition (velocity), and acceleration dynamics. Our framework is rigorous in the sense that our NDFs are constructed on the product space of joint rotations, their angular velocities, and angular accelerations, respecting the geometry of the underlying articulations. We further introduce (i) a novel adaptive-step hybrid algorithm for projecting onto the set of plausible motions, and (ii) a novel geometric integrator to roll out realistic motion trajectories during test-time optimization and generation. Our experiments show significant and consistent gains across multiple input modalities and tasks.

Method Overview

NRMF is a general-purpose, expressive and robust unconditional motion prior. It models the space of plausible poses (\u03B8), transitions (\u02D9\u03B8), and accelerations (\u02DD\u03B8) on the zero-level set of a geometric neural distance field. Poses are depicted alongside transitions and accelerations, which are visualized as blue dots and rings showing the learned distributions. We develop projection (\u03A0) and integration algorithms for denoising, in-the-wild estimation, in-betweening and generation.

Applications & Demos

Motion Denoising & Infilling from Noisy / Partial Observations

We recover clean and plausible motion from noisy or partial 3D observations and can infill missing body parts or in-between the motion.

Motion Estimation on In-the-wild Videos

Robust reconstruction from RGB-D / RGB videos in diverse scenes (e.g., PROX, EgoBody, 3DPW, and in-the-wild).

Motion In-betweening & Generation

In-between plausible motion from sparse keyframes and generate natural motion from initial poses, while remaining temporally consistent and physically plausible.
