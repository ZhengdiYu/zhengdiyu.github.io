Zhengdi Yu

I am a PhD student at the Department of Computing, CIRCLE Group, Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Tolga Birdal and working with Prof. Stefanos Zafeiriou. Before that, I finished my MRes degree in Computer Science at Durham University under the supervision of Prof. Toby Breckon. During my MRes, I was also a research intern at Tencent AI Lab, Visual Computing Centre, working with Dr. Shaoli Huang and Dr. Jue Wang.

My research interests primarily focus on 3D computer vision, with a particular emphasis on 4D motion representations of articulated bodies, such as hands and human bodies, as well as parametric models. I am also interested in 3D shape reconstruction and scene understanding towards applications for real-world problems.
